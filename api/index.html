API reference#

This reference lists the docstrings of the public names in Bramble.jl's core library, grouped by the stage of a computation they belong to. The SciML, automatic differentiation and solver integrations are on Scientific computing: SciML, AD and solvers. A typical program follows the pages in order. It describes the domain, builds a mesh on it, defines a grid space, applies difference operators, and assembles and solves a form. If you are new to the library, start with the tutorials and come here to look up a signature or an option.

  • Utilities: linear algebra backends and other package-wide helpers.

  • Geometry: sets, intervals, markers and domains, which describe where a problem lives and how its boundary is labelled.

  • Meshes: mesh types and constructors, points and spacings, indexing and boundaries, and adaptation.

  • Grid spaces: function spaces on a mesh, their degrees of freedom, grid functions, restriction and averaging, and interpolation between spaces.

  • Difference, jump and average operators: the discrete derivatives and related operators that act on grid functions.

  • Inner products and norms: discrete inner products and the norms built from them.

  • Forms: building, assembling and solving bilinear and linear forms, including matrix-free operators, Dirichlet conditions and sparsity analysis.

  • Exporters: writing grid functions to files for visualisation.
